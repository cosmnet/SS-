Capture, annotate, share — without leaving the notch.

SS+ lives in your menu bar and in a dynamic island under the notch. One shortcut for area, window or full-screen capture; draw on it in place; record video; optionally upload to your own Google Drive with a share link on your clipboard.

Download for macOS What's new

macOS 14 Sonoma or later · Apple Silicon · Free, no account required. The app is not notarized yet: on first launch, use System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

Features

Island interface

A compact toolbar that rests in the notch and opens on hover. Fully optional; it only appears during capture and recording if you turn it off.

Instant capture

⌃⌘– opens the last-used mode. Area drag (⇧ for square), window click with smart detection, or ↩ for the entire screen.

Annotate in place

Pen, highlighter, arrows, shapes and text right on top of the capture. Undo/redo, keyboard tools 1–7, save plain or annotated.

Video recording

ScreenCaptureKit recording to MOV or MP4 with H.264 — HEVC, 60 fps, custom bit rate and system audio in Pro. Draw on screen while recording.

Preview card

After every capture a small card appears: edit, copy, OCR (Pro), reveal, delete, or drag the file straight into another app.

Google Drive (optional)

Sign in with your own Google account. Screenshots (and optionally recordings) upload to an SS+ folder in your Drive; a public link can be copied automatically.

Clipboard history (opt-in)

Keep the last 10 copies handy in the island and the menu panel. Off by default; concealed content from password managers is never stored.

Turkish & English

The whole interface follows your system language, or pick one in Settings. Changes apply instantly.

Privacy first

SS+ has no servers and no analytics. Everything stays on your Mac unless you turn on Google Drive upload — and even then, the app only gets access to the folder and files it creates (Google's narrowest drive.file scope), your sign-in tokens stay in your Keychain, and the developer never sees your files.
