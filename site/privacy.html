Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 August 2026 · Applies to the SS+ macOS application ("SS+", "the app", "we").

SS+ is designed to keep your data on your own Mac. This policy explains what the app stores, what it sends, and the choices you have.

1. Summary

  • SS+ runs entirely on your computer. It has no accounts, no servers of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting.
  • Nothing you capture leaves your Mac. SS+ has no server to send it to.
  • The developer never receives your screenshots, recordings, clipboard contents, Google credentials or any other personal data.

2. Data the app stores on your Mac

Screenshots and recordings

Captures are written to the folder you choose in Settings (the Desktop by default). They are ordinary files under your control; deleting them in Finder or from the preview card removes them.

Settings

Preferences (shortcuts, formats, language, folder path, feature toggles) are stored in macOS user defaults for the app.

Clipboard history (opt-in, off by default)

If you enable it, the last ten items you copy (text up to 5,000 characters and images) are saved to ~/Library/Application Support/SSPlus/Clipboard so they can be shown in the island and the menu panel. Content that other apps mark as concealed or transient (for example password managers) is never stored. You can clear the archive at any time, choose to clear it automatically on quit, or turn the feature off.

Google sign-in tokens

4. Update check

Once a day (and when you choose Check for updates), the app downloads a small version file from this website to tell you whether a newer version exists. The request contains no personal data and nothing about you is recorded.

5. macOS permissions

SS+ needs Screen & System Audio Recording permission to capture the screen; macOS asks for it on first use. The app uses it only while you take a screenshot or record. The Uninstall / Reset permissions option in Settings removes the permission records for a clean reinstall.

6. Data sharing and sale

We do not collect, share or sell personal data. SS+ has no account system and no analytics; there is nothing for us to collect.

7. Children

SS+ is a general-purpose utility not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

8. Deleting your data

Delete captured files in Finder. Use Settings → About → Uninstall / Reset permissions → Uninstall completely to remove settings, the clipboard archive and the app itself.

9. Changes to this policy

If the policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated effective date.
