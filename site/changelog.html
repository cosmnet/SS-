Version history

What's new in each version of SS+. Get the latest from the home page, or use “Check for updates” in the app.

1.6.6 26 August 2026

  • Signed and notarised by Apple. macOS no longer blocks the download with “the app could not be opened”. No more right-clicking or Terminal commands on first launch.
  • Redesigned the notch island. It now covers the notch and lays its content out on either side of it, instead of dropping everything below the notch on top of whatever app you were using. Sized from your Mac’s real notch measurements, so it fits MacBook Pro and MacBook Air alike.
  • All permissions on one screen. Instead of separate system prompts at unpredictable moments, SS+ shows what it needs, why it needs it, and which parts are optional — and you can skip it.
  • Google Drive upload has a proper on/off switch in Settings. Turning it off stops uploads without signing you out.
  • Fixed a crash in the English interface. A duplicate entry in the English text table crashed the app the first time it was read — on the menu bar click, the shortcut and the first-launch permission window. The permission window also opened far taller than the screen, hiding its buttons.
  • Settings now shows the detected notch size, with a button that draws where SS+ thinks the notch is.

1.6.5 26 August 2026

  • Fixed for real this time: the memory that stayed high after screenshots. Captured images are now fully handed back to the system right after they're saved, and SS+ returns its scratch memory to macOS after each capture.

1.6.4 26 August 2026

  • Fixed: memory no longer jumps by 100 MB or more after each screenshot. The full-size capture is written to disk and to the clipboard and then released; the preview card keeps only a small thumbnail, and Edit / OCR read the saved file when you use them.
  • Pasting into other apps works as before (PNG/JPEG, with TIFF provided on request).

1.6.3 26 August 2026

  • Uses much less memory. SS+ no longer keeps your last screenshot in memory after the preview card closes, and the island and Settings window are only kept around while they're on screen. Typical idle use drops from around 200 MB after a few captures to under 50 MB.
  • Video recording uses less memory while it runs.

1.6.2 26 August 2026

  • Fixed: taking a screenshot of SS+'s own Settings window now captures the window itself — it used to capture whatever was behind it.

1.6.1 26 August 2026

  • Microphone recording now works on every Mac that SS+ supports, not only on the newest macOS.
  • SS+ asks for microphone access the moment you turn on audio recording, so your first recording isn't interrupted by a permission prompt.
  • Settings now tells you when something isn't available on your version of macOS (for example, system audio needs macOS 13).
  • Small fixes and polish.

1.6.0 25 August 2026

  • Audio recording (Pro) is now one switch. Turn on “Record audio” and both your Mac's sound and your microphone are saved with the video, as two separate audio tracks you can mix or mute later. On by default with Pro.
  • Live sound level in the island while you record, so you can see that audio is actually being picked up.
  • Fixed: recording a screen where nothing moves could fail with “no frames captured”. Those recordings are saved now.
  • The island stays hidden while you're not capturing, so your notch and menu bar stay quiet. You can show it again in Settings → General.
  • “Check for updates” no longer shows a new-version alert when you're already up to date.
  • Permission and uninstall messages are now fully available in both Turkish and English.

1.5.0 25 August 2026

  • Runs on older Macs. SS+ now works from macOS 10.15 Catalina on Intel Macs and macOS 11 Big Sur on Apple Silicon — one download for both.
  • Launch at login works on all supported versions.
  • On older versions of macOS some things look simpler (the notch island needs macOS 12 or later), but every capture and recording feature is there.

1.4.0 25 August 2026

  • Google Drive: reinstalling SS+ or signing in again now reuses your existing SS+ folder instead of creating a new one.
  • SS+ starts at login by default (you can turn this off in Settings → General).
  • Check for updates: SS+ tells you when a new version is out and can open this page. It checks once a day; you can turn that off.
  • New website with the privacy policy, terms and this version history; links to it after connecting Google Drive and in Settings.
  • The app version is shown in the menu bar menu and in Settings → About.

1.3.0 25 August 2026

  • Google Drive upload. Sign in with your own Google account and every screenshot goes to an SS+ folder in your Drive. Optionally share each file with “anyone with the link” and have the link copied to your clipboard automatically.
  • Optional upload of video recordings to Drive too (off by default).
  • “Uninstall / Reset permissions” in Settings for a clean reinstall.
  • A clearer “Google Drive connected” page after you sign in.
  • Redesigned Settings: tabs in the title bar and a more compact layout.
  • Fixed: the Screen Recording permission no longer disappears after updating SS+.
  • The drawing editor no longer opens automatically after every capture; turn it on in Settings → General if you prefer that.

1.2.0 25 August 2026

  • English interface. Choose System, Türkçe or English in Settings → General — it applies instantly.
  • Taller Settings window so nothing needs scrolling.

1.1.0 25 August 2026

  • Fixed: quitting SS+ or a sudden interruption (display sleep, lock screen) during a recording no longer leaves you with an unplayable video — the recording is saved up to that point.
  • Fixed: the recording shortcut and menu item now also stop a recording.
  • Clipboard history is now off by default and has a “clear on quit” option.
  • New default shortcuts: ⌃⌘– to capture, ⌃⇧⌘– to record, so ⌘– keeps working as “Zoom out” in your other apps.
  • New video settings: file type (MOV/MP4), quality, bit rate, system audio, and whether the cursor is included.

1.0.0 24 August 2026

  • First release: the island under the notch, area / window / full-screen capture, drawing on captures, video recording, the preview card, clipboard strip, and Pro (text recognition, 60 fps, system audio).
